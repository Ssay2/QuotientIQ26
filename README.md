## What QuotientIQ Is

QuotientIQ is a platform where companies can:

- Discover AI agents
- Buy or subscribe to AI workflows
- Automate operations
- Connect company tools and data
- Build custom AI employees
- Monitor performance and security
- Scale AI across departments

It combines ideas from:

- OpenAI (AI intelligence)
- Salesforce (enterprise workflows)
- Zapier (automation)
- AWS Marketplace (marketplace infrastructure)
- Microsoft Copilot (AI assistants)
- ServiceNow (enterprise operations)

But focuses specifically on AI agents that work like digital employees for companies.

## Core Vision

Every company in the world will eventually have AI workers.

QuotientIQ aims to become the place where companies:

- Hire AI agents
- Train AI agents
- Manage AI agents
- Buy AI automation
- Build internal AI systems
- Connect AI into existing business software

## Problems We Solve

### 1. Massive Operational Waste

Teams lose time on repetitive tasks such as emails, spreadsheets, data entry, scheduling, reporting, support, research, and onboarding.

### 2. AI Adoption Is Hard

Most companies do not know:

- How to use AI
- Which tools to trust
- How to integrate AI
- How to secure AI
- How to train AI systems

### 3. AI Stack Fragmentation

Businesses juggle disconnected tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Zapier, Notion AI, Salesforce AI, internal systems).

QuotientIQ acts as the operating system layer for enterprise AI.

Third-party developers build and publish agents to QuotientIQ.
