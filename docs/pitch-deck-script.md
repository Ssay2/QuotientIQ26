# QuotientIQ Pitch Deck Script (15 Slides)

## Slide 1 - Title

**Headline:** QuotientIQ
**Subheadline:** Enterprise AI Agent Marketplace and Operating System
**Speaker notes:**
QuotientIQ helps businesses deploy AI workers across every core function. We are building the operating layer where companies discover, deploy, and govern AI agents at scale.

## Slide 2 - Problem

**Headline:** Businesses are overloaded with repetitive work and fragmented AI tools
**Bullets:**
- High-value teams still spend time on repetitive workflows
- AI adoption is technical, risky, and hard to govern
- Companies use disconnected AI products with no central control
**Speaker notes:**
Today, companies are not blocked by awareness of AI. They are blocked by reliable execution. There is no single enterprise-grade layer to operationalize AI workers safely and measurably.

## Slide 3 - Why Current Options Are Not Enough

**Headline:** Point solutions do not solve operational AI execution
**Bullets:**
- Copilots assist individuals but do not run business workflows end to end
- Automation tools often lack robust AI orchestration and governance
- Teams still patch together stacks with high integration overhead
**Speaker notes:**
Businesses need a system of execution, not only a chat interface or isolated automations. They need a platform that works across tools, teams, and policies.

## Slide 4 - Solution

**Headline:** QuotientIQ is the operating system for enterprise AI workers
**Bullets:**
- AI agent marketplace for discovery and subscription
- Workflow engine for repeatable business automation
- Admin dashboard for performance, spend, and governance
- Integration layer for CRM, support, collaboration, and finance tools
**Speaker notes:**
We unify AI agent discovery, deployment, orchestration, and oversight in one platform. This turns AI from experimentation into operational infrastructure.

## Slide 5 - Product Walkthrough

**Headline:** From install to business impact in minutes
**Bullets:**
- Install an AI support workflow from marketplace
- Connect email, CRM, and ticket data sources
- Configure policy and approval thresholds
- Launch workflow and monitor resolution metrics
**Speaker notes:**
Our product is designed for speed and trust. Operators can deploy production workflows without deep AI engineering knowledge.

## Slide 6 - Beachhead MVP

**Headline:** Start narrow: AI Workflow Marketplace for SMBs
**Bullets:**
- Customer support automation
- Lead generation workflows
- Email follow-up automation
- Reporting and KPI summaries
**Speaker notes:**
We intentionally begin with high-frequency, measurable workflows that create fast ROI and short feedback loops.

## Slide 7 - Market Opportunity

**Headline:** AI execution infrastructure is a large, expanding category
**Bullets:**
- AI software budgets are increasing across SMB and enterprise
- Operational automation is a persistent, budgeted problem
- Marketplace models scale with demand and developer supply
**Speaker notes:**
The shift is from AI as assistant to AI as worker. The control layer for that shift is the core market opportunity.

## Slide 8 - Business Model

**Headline:** Multi-stream recurring revenue model
**Bullets:**
- SaaS plans: Starter, Pro, Enterprise
- Marketplace take rate: 15% to 30%
- Enterprise contracts: setup, integration, private deployments
- Usage-based revenue for requests and workflow volume
**Speaker notes:**
This model combines predictable subscriptions with scalable marketplace and usage economics.

## Slide 9 - Go-To-Market

**Headline:** Land with focused workflows, expand by ROI
**Bullets:**
- Founder-led sales to early SMB operators
- Productized onboarding and implementation playbooks
- Expand from single use case to cross-team deployment
- Open third-party publishing once demand is validated
**Speaker notes:**
Our GTM is pragmatic: win with one painful workflow, prove ROI fast, then grow within account and ecosystem.

## Slide 10 - Competition

**Headline:** Strong incumbents, clear white space
**Bullets:**
- Direct: Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Einstein AI, UiPath
- Indirect: Zapier, Notion AI, Monday.com AI
- White space: marketplace + workflow execution + governance in one system
**Speaker notes:**
We differentiate by combining a two-sided marketplace with enterprise execution and governance, not just assistance.

## Slide 11 - Defensibility

**Headline:** Durable moat from data, ecosystem, and integrations
**Bullets:**
- Network effects from developers and buyers
- Proprietary workflow templates and performance benchmarks
- Deep integration graph and switching costs
- Governance trust layer for enterprise adoption
**Speaker notes:**
As usage grows, the platform gets smarter and harder to replace. Defensibility compounds with every successful deployment.

## Slide 12 - Roadmap

**Headline:** Sequence: prove value first, then scale platform
**Bullets:**
- Phase 1: MVP with 4 workflow categories
- Phase 2: More connectors and analytics depth
- Phase 3: Open marketplace publishing
- Phase 4: Enterprise governance and private deployment
**Speaker notes:**
Execution sequencing is critical. We avoid broad feature sprawl and prioritize monetizable functionality.

## Slide 13 - Milestones

**Headline:** What we need to prove in the next 12-18 months
**Bullets:**
- MVP launch with production-grade reliability baseline
- First 10 paying customers
- First meaningful MRR milestone
- Marketplace launch with external creators
- First enterprise pilot
**Speaker notes:**
These milestones de-risk product, demand, and platform economics in a staged, measurable way.

## Slide 14 - Fundraise Ask

**Headline:** Capital to accelerate product, GTM, and trust layer
**Bullets:**
- Build core product and integration roadmap
- Expand workflow quality and reliability systems
- Strengthen security, compliance, and governance foundations
- Scale GTM and customer success
**Speaker notes:**
We will deploy capital into high-leverage functions that directly improve conversion, retention, and platform defensibility.

## Slide 15 - Closing

**Headline:** QuotientIQ will be where businesses deploy AI workers
**Speaker notes:**
The future is not AI chat alone. The future is AI execution at enterprise scale. QuotientIQ is building the infrastructure layer for that future.
