# QuotientIQ Investor One-Pager

## Company

QuotientIQ is an enterprise AI agent marketplace and operating system that helps businesses deploy AI workers across departments.

## Vision

Every company will eventually have AI workers. QuotientIQ aims to become the default platform where organizations hire, train, manage, and scale those AI workers.

## Problem

Businesses face three major blockers:

1. Repetitive operational work consumes expensive human time.
2. AI adoption is difficult due to integration, trust, and security complexity.
3. AI tools are fragmented across disconnected products.

## Solution

QuotientIQ centralizes enterprise AI operations in one platform:

- AI agent marketplace for discovery and subscription
- No-code workflow automation for business tasks
- Private agent builder trained on internal company knowledge
- Integrations across business systems (CRM, support, collaboration, finance)
- Governance, analytics, and security controls for enterprise adoption

## Why Now

- Foundation model quality and reliability are improving quickly.
- Companies are under pressure to reduce cost and increase output.
- Enterprises are shifting from AI chat interfaces to AI execution systems.
- Marketplace models create durable defensibility via network effects.

## Product Scope (MVP)

Initial wedge: AI Workflow Marketplace for SMBs.

Launch use cases:

- Customer support automation
- Lead generation workflows
- Email automation
- Reporting assistants

MVP stack:

- Frontend: Next.js, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js, Express
- Auth: Clerk or Auth0
- Data: PostgreSQL
- AI APIs: OpenAI and Anthropic
- Payments: Stripe
- Hosting: Vercel

## Business Model

1. SaaS subscriptions (Starter, Pro, Enterprise)
2. Marketplace revenue share (target 15% to 30%)
3. Enterprise setup and deployment contracts
4. Usage-based billing for API, compute, and workflow volume
5. White-label enterprise deployments

## Go-To-Market

1. Start with a narrow verticalized workflow bundle for SMBs.
2. Convert early adopters through direct founder-led sales and implementation support.
3. Expand to multi-team deployment inside accounts.
4. Open marketplace publishing to third-party developers.
5. Move upmarket with governance and compliance packages.

## Target Customers

- SMBs seeking affordable automation
- Mid-market companies scaling operations
- Enterprises requiring compliance and governance
- Agencies building AI operations for clients
- Developers monetizing production-ready agents

## Moat and Defensibility

- Marketplace network effects (more agents -> more buyers -> more developers)
- Proprietary workflow templates and automation playbooks
- Deep enterprise integrations and switching costs
- Aggregated performance data and optimization feedback loops

## Traction Milestones (Suggested)

- Milestone 1: MVP live with 3 to 5 high-value workflows
- Milestone 2: First 10 paying SMB accounts
- Milestone 3: First $10K MRR
- Milestone 4: Marketplace launch with external developers
- Milestone 5: First enterprise pilot and security review

## Key Risks

1. Competition from large incumbents
2. Long enterprise sales cycles
3. AI reliability and hallucination risk
4. Rising infrastructure costs
5. Security and compliance execution complexity

## What Success Looks Like

QuotientIQ becomes the control layer for enterprise AI execution, not just chat. If executed well, it can evolve into core infrastructure for AI workers across business operations.
